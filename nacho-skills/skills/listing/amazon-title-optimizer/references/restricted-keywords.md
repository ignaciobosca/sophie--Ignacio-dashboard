# Amazon Restricted Keywords Reference (Sophie Society)

Complete Sophie Society restricted-keyword list. Keywords Amazon restricts in listing copy, A+
content, and infographics. A 🚩 means the keyword is **restricted** in that area; ✅ means allowed.

**This list is complete and bundled into the skill — nothing here needs editing or refreshing to
run.** For titles + Item Highlights, only the **Listing Copy** column matters: a keyword is a
violation when its **Listing Copy** cell is 🚩 **and** the ASIN's **category** is listed in
*Categories Restricted* **or** the row applies to **General** (General applies to every category).

## Key rules to remember

- **Disease / condition names** (Cancer, Diabetes, HIV, Depression, etc.) — restricted across ALL
  listing areas in General and Health & Household.
- **Competitor references** and **™ ® TM R** symbols/marks — restricted universally in copy.
- **"The Best", "Free", "Cheap", "Guarantee", "Affordable"** and other promotional terms —
  restricted in General (also enforced by `copy-rules.md`).
- **Environmental claims** (Biodegradable, Recyclable, Eco-friendly, Sustainable) — restricted in
  specific categories; check per product.
- **Controlled substances** (Cocaine, Cannabis, Heroin, etc.) — universally restricted.
- The single-letter entries **"R"** and **"$"** mean those characters as standalone marks/symbols;
  in titles they are already blocked by the allowed-character rule in `copy-rules.md`.
