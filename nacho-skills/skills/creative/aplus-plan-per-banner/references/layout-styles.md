# Layout Styles Reference (100)

Use this library to pick the `[Layout]` value for each card's image-generation prompt.
Choose a layout that fits the card's job (e.g. Founder Highlight → a person/portrait layout;
Product Showcase → a product-hero layout; Quote + Powerful Visual → big-typography layout).
Cite the layout by name in the prompt so the designer/model knows the composition intent.

Layouts 1–40 are product-only compositions. Layouts 41–100 involve a person, hands, or lifestyle context.

| # | Layout Name | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zoomed Product Corner Layout | Product is oversized, slightly cropped, and placed in one corner. Text sits in the empty area. |
| 2 | Tilted Floating Product Layout | Product is slightly rotated and floating on a plain one-color background. Text can sit beside it or follow the angle. |
| 3 | Product Half-Off-Canvas Layout | Only part of the product is visible, entering from the side or bottom. The rest of the image stays clean for bold copy. |
| 4 | Centered Product With Orbit Callouts | Product is centered, with callouts arranged around it in a circular/orbit style using thin lines. |
| 5 | Diagonal Split Composition | The image is divided diagonally. Product sits on one side, text on the other. Background can use two tones of the same color. |
| 6 | Big Typography + Small Product Layout | The headline is the main visual element. Product is smaller and placed in a corner, bottom area, or beside the text. |
| 7 | Macro Detail Crop Layout | A close-up product detail fills most of the image, with a small full product cutout in the corner. |
| 8 | Text Card Overlay Layout | Product sits on a plain background, with text placed inside floating cards, rounded rectangles, or clean info boxes. |
| 9 | Product on Pedestal / Platform Layout | Product sits on a simple geometric platform, block, circle, or shadow shape. Text callouts are placed around it. |
| 10 | Left Product / Right Text Stack | Product takes 50–60% of the left side. The right side has stacked benefits, bullets, or feature cards. |
| 11 | Right Product / Left Editorial Headline | Product sits on the right. A large headline and short supporting text sit on the left. |
| 12 | Top Product / Bottom Information Bar | Product is large in the upper area. A clean horizontal bar at the bottom contains icons, specs, or short callouts. |
| 13 | Bottom Product / Top Bold Claim | Product sits at the bottom, often slightly cropped. A large benefit headline fills the top half. |
| 14 | Repeating Product Pattern Background | A subtle repeated product silhouette, texture, or detail sits in the background, with one main product clearly shown in front. |
| 15 | Layered Depth Layout | Product is in the foreground with soft background shapes behind it, such as circles, blobs, gradients, shadows, or layered panels. |
| 16 | Corner Badge + Main Product Layout | Product is centered or side-positioned, with a strong badge in one corner for one key claim. |
| 17 | Close-Up Strip Layout | One side of the image has a vertical or horizontal strip showing close-up details. The main product sits beside it. |
| 18 | Minimal Luxury Layout | Clean background, product slightly off-center, lots of empty space, small elegant copy, and very restrained callouts. |
| 19 | Angled Product With Arrow Flow | Product is placed diagonally, with arrows or motion lines following the direction of use. |
| 20 | Boxed Sections Layout | Image is divided into 2–4 rectangular content zones. Product overlaps the boxes slightly so the design feels less flat. |
| 21 | Floating Product With Shadow | Product floats in the center or side of the image with a soft drop shadow underneath. Text is minimal and clean. |
| 22 | Product With Large Background Shape | Product is placed over a circle, arch, oval, blob, or rectangle that creates contrast behind it. |
| 23 | Product Cutout + Icon Row | Product is the main hero, with a simple row of icons underneath or beside it. |
| 24 | Product With Numbered Callouts | Visible product features are labeled with small numbers, with a legend on the side explaining each point. |
| 25 | Product Detail Bubble Layout | Main product is shown full-size, with circular zoom bubbles highlighting specific features. |
| 26 | Product Grid Layout | The product appears in multiple angles inside a 2x2 or 3-panel grid. |
| 27 | Product Angle Trio | Three product angles are layered together: front, side, and detail crop. Text sits in the remaining white space. |
| 28 | Product With Packaging Layout | Product is shown next to its box, pouch, label, or included packaging. Good for giftable or premium products. |
| 29 | Exploded View Layout | The product is separated into parts or layers to show construction, materials, or what is included. |
| 30 | Flat Lay Product Layout | Product and accessories are arranged neatly from above on a clean background. |
| 31 | Symmetrical Center Hero Layout | Product is perfectly centered, with balanced text or icons on both sides. |
| 32 | Product With Measurement Lines | Product is shown with dimension lines, arrows, or scale markers around it. |
| 33 | Product in Frame Layout | Product is placed inside a simple frame, arch, window, or border, with text outside or partially overlapping. |
| 34 | Product + Texture Background Layout | A macro texture from the product becomes the background, while the full product sits in front. |
| 35 | Product With Color Block Layout | Background is split into bold color blocks or soft panels, with product overlapping the color areas. |
| 36 | Product Floating Over Label Cards | Product appears above or between 2–4 text cards that describe the main benefits. |
| 37 | Product With Circular Badge System | Multiple circular badges surround the product, each containing one short benefit or feature. |
| 38 | Product Shadow Silhouette Layout | A soft silhouette or outline of the product sits behind the main product to add depth. |
| 39 | Product With Directional Motion Lines | Motion lines show movement, airflow, cutting direction, pulling, pressing, opening, carrying, or rotation. |
| 40 | Product With "Inside View" Cutaway | Product is shown open or partially cut away to reveal the interior, storage, layers, or construction. |
| 41 | Hand Holding Product + Floating Callouts | A hand holds the product against a plain background. Text callouts float around the hand/product. |
| 42 | Person Holding Product, Cropped Body | Only part of the person is visible, such as hands, torso, arm, or shoulder. The face is not shown, so the product stays the focus. |
| 43 | Over-the-Shoulder Product View | Camera is positioned behind or beside the person, showing the product in use from their perspective. |
| 44 | Hands in Action Layout | Two hands actively use the product. Text is placed around the action area. |
| 45 | One Hand Reaching Into Product | A hand reaches into the product to show access, storage, organization, or ease of use. |
| 46 | Person Using Product + Side Text Panel | Lifestyle/action image takes 60–70% of the layout. A clean text panel sits on one side. |
| 47 | Cropped Lifestyle Corner | A lifestyle usage scene sits in one corner, while the rest of the image stays plain for copy. |
| 48 | Product in Hand, Plain Color Background | A clean hand-held product shot on a solid background, with headline and 2–3 short callouts. |
| 49 | Before / After With Person | One side shows the person struggling without the product. The other side shows them using it easily. |
| 50 | Person + Product Close-Up Crop | Tight crop on hand/product interaction so the product feels large and detailed. |
| 51 | Person Wearing / Carrying Product | Product is shown on the body or being carried naturally. |
| 52 | Product Scale in Hand | Product is shown in a hand to communicate size immediately. |
| 53 | Hand Pointing to Feature | A finger points to a key detail, and the label explains it. |
| 54 | Person Opening Product | Person opens the product so the viewer can see the interior, contents, lid, zipper, case, or compartments. |
| 55 | Person Carrying Product in Real Context | Person is walking, cooking, gardening, cleaning, traveling, or working with the product. Text is minimal. |
| 56 | Hands + Step Numbers | Each hand action is paired with a number. |
| 57 | Product Held Toward Camera | Person holds the product closer to the lens, so it appears large in the foreground while the person/background is softer. |
| 58 | Person in Background, Product in Foreground | Product is sharp and large in front. Person using it or benefiting from it is slightly blurred in the background. |
| 59 | Hand Pulling / Lifting / Pressing Motion | A hand gesture shows the main action of the product. |
| 60 | Product Passed Between Hands | Product is shown being handed from one person to another. |
| 61 | Person Holding Product With Big Empty Space | Person/product is placed on one side, leaving a large plain area for headline and benefits. |
| 62 | Hands Framing the Product | Hands naturally frame the product from both sides, making it feel tactile and important. |
| 63 | Person Demonstrating Size Comparison | Person holds the product next to a familiar object or body part for scale. |
| 64 | Person Showing Product Interior | Person tilts the product slightly toward the camera so the inside is visible. |
| 65 | Hand + Macro Detail Bubble | Main image shows person holding/using the product, while a small zoom bubble highlights the detail. |
| 66 | Person Using Product With Minimal Icons | Lifestyle photo is used as the base, with clean icons placed around the product. |
| 67 | Hands on Plain Studio Background | Only hands are shown on a solid-color background. No full lifestyle environment. |
| 68 | Person Silhouette + Product Focus | A subtle person silhouette or softly lit body shape sits behind the product. The product remains the hero. |
| 69 | Cropped Face Reaction Layout | Lower face, smile, or expression is visible while holding/using the product. Text explains the emotional benefit. |
| 70 | Product in Use With Label Lines | Person uses the product naturally, and thin label lines point to visible features. |
| 71 | Person Holding Two Options | Person holds the old solution in one hand and your product in the other. |
| 72 | POV Hands Layout | Camera shows the user's own hands using the product, as if the viewer is doing it themselves. |
| 73 | Person Carrying Product + Motion Trail | Product is shown being carried or moved, with subtle motion lines or arrows. |
| 74 | Person Holding Product Over Clean Surface | Hands hold the product above a table, counter, desk, or clean surface. Text sits around the edges. |
| 75 | Product Held Beside Packaging | Person holds the product in one hand and packaging, accessories, or included items beside it. |
| 76 | Hand Hovering Above Product | Hand is about to touch, open, press, or pick up the product. Good for creating anticipation and action. |
| 77 | Hand Placing Product Down | Shows the product being placed on a counter, shelf, desk, garden bed, or table. |
| 78 | Hand Removing Product From Box | Unboxing-style composition showing the product being taken out of packaging. |
| 79 | Person Packing Product | Person places items into the product to show capacity or organization. |
| 80 | Person Cleaning / Maintaining Product | Person wipes, rinses, folds, stores, or cleans the product to show easy maintenance. |
| 81 | Person Holding Product at Chest Level | Product is held naturally in front of the body, giving clear scale and a casual lifestyle feel. |
| 82 | Person Holding Product at Waist Level | Useful for bags, tools, kitchen items, and anything carried while working. |
| 83 | Person Holding Product Over Shoulder | Best for shoulder bags, straps, garden totes, travel bags, sports gear, and wearable accessories. |
| 84 | Person Using Product From Side Profile | Side view of the person interacting with the product, with empty space on the opposite side for copy. |
| 85 | Hands Showing Texture | Hands touch or pinch the product material to show softness, flexibility, grip, thickness, or durability. |
| 86 | Hand Pulling Strap / Handle | Close-up of a hand pulling a strap, handle, grip, loop, latch, or zipper to show strength and function. |
| 87 | Person Holding Product in Outdoor Context | Person holds or uses the product outside, while text remains clean and minimal. |
| 88 | Person Using Product in Kitchen Context | Person uses product on a counter, cutting board, sink, pantry, or table. |
| 89 | Person Using Product in Bathroom / Beauty Context | Person uses product near a mirror, sink, vanity, shower, or shelf. |
| 90 | Person Using Product in Workspace Context | Person uses product on a desk, studio table, workshop bench, or office setup. |
| 91 | Person Using Product With Pet | Person interacts with the product while the pet is present or benefiting from it. |
| 92 | Person Holding Product Beside Child / Family Context | Useful when the product is for parents, children, home life, or family routines. |
| 93 | Person Showing Grip Comfort | Close-up of hand gripping the product, with text explaining comfort, control, or reduced fatigue. |
| 94 | Hand Pressing Button / Mechanism | Close-up of the hand using a key mechanism. |
| 95 | Hands Folding / Collapsing Product | Shows compact storage or portability. |
| 96 | Hands Assembling Product | Shows setup, installation, or easy assembly. |
| 97 | Person Holding Product With Highlight Glow | Product is held naturally, but the product has a soft outline, glow, or spotlight effect to make it stand out. |
| 98 | Person Using Product With Split Plain Background | Person/product cutout is placed on a plain color block background, not a full lifestyle photo. |
| 99 | Product in Hand With Large Benefit Word | Hand/product sits on one side with one huge word behind or beside it. |
| 100 | Hands + Product + Mini Comparison | Hands hold the product while a small inset shows the old solution or competitor for contrast. |
