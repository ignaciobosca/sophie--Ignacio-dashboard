Profitability & Financial Health

COGS-aware net profit · Margins · ROI · Cost waterfall

—
This P&L is settlement-basis. Revenue, fees, promotions and refunds come from Amazon’s settled financial events, so closed-month totals reconcile to your payouts (same basis as Sellerboard). Figures here can differ from the Business Overview, which uses ordered sales (demand). Why: (1) ordered vs shipped/settled timing — a sale ordered late in a month settles in the next; (2) the Overview counts ad-attributed sales on an order basis; (3) refunds & reserves settle later. The current month & daily figures use order data (settlement posts ~2 weeks later), so they’re directional until the month closes.
Net = Sales − Ad − Amazon fees − Promo − Refunds − COGS · Est. payout = Sales − fees − promo − refunds − ad · Revenue = ordered sales; fees/refunds = settled (financialEvents)

Manual data & backfill override pulled months or add months the API didn’t return

Two uses: (1) override a pulled month/field (marked ✎), and (2) backfill months the API never returned — use + Add month (or just include the month in a CSV). The common case is ads: ad reports have a shorter lookback than sales/financials, so older months come through with $0 ad spend — add the month (or edit it) and type the ad figure. Columns: Month, Sales, Units, Ad, Referral, FBA, Other, Storage, Promo, Refunds. Everything flows into Net & the whole P&L. Added months are account-level only (no per-product split). Per-unit COGS has its own editor on the Products tab. Entries stay while this dashboard is open; a Cowork live-refresh rebuilds from the feed, so ask Claude to bake recurring overrides into the build.
